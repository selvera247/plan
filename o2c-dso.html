    Step 2 · Measure

    Days sales outstanding

    Use a count-back DSO so one large invoice doesn’t distort the picture the way a simple AR ÷ average daily sales can.

    Formula we use

    Start with ending AR. Subtract the most recent months’ credit sales until AR is covered. DSO = days in those full months + the fraction of the next month needed.

    What “good” looks like

    • Contract terms: mostly Net 30 → structural floor near ~30 days once billed.
    • Target DSO: ≤ 38 days (terms + ~1 week of collection lag).
    • Gap to chase: —

    DSO ignores unbilled WIP. If work-to-bill is slow, cash still lags even when DSO looks fine.

    Baselining checklist

    1. Draw the map

      Interview billing + delivery once. Write the real stages (not the handbook)—especially where AI/tooling cost waits on a monthly invoice.

    2. Timestamp each stage

      Pull last 20 invoices: work complete date → invoice date → payment date. Medians become your baseline.

    3. Lock DSO + WIP + overdue

      One snapshot in the close pack every month. DSO moves the bill-to-cash lever; WIP aging moves work-to-bill.
