Operating rhythm

What “rolling” means in practice

  1. Monday open

    Lock actual ending cash from the bank. Replace last week’s forecast with actuals in a variance column (keep history off this sheet).

  2. Shift the window

    Delete the week that just ended. Add a new week 13 at the far right. Re-age AR: what you expected to collect this week either arrived or slides forward.

  3. Refresh drivers

    Update invoice dates, milestone billings, payroll calendar, contractor draws, and AI/cloud budgets. Flag any week that falls under the buffer.

Sample setup

Assumptions behind this model

  • Inflows

    Mix of T&M collections (Net 30), one fixed-fee milestone, and a retainer. No new sales assumed beyond work already in flight.

  • Outflows

    Biweekly payroll, contractors billed weekly, rent on the 1st of the month week, and variable LLM/API spend tagged to delivery.

  • Buffer

    $250k target ≈ roughly four weeks of core burn. Weeks under the line are the ones that force collections or spend cuts.
