First eight hours

Today’s agenda

Finish with three numbers you trust: opening cash, unbilled WIP, and overdue AR—and a named owner for revenue vs. cost.

  1. 09:00

    Access & people

    Bank portal, GL / billing tool, payroll, expense system, LLM admin or usage export. Meet the other finance seat—agree who owns revenue vs. cost (segregation of duties).

  2. 10:30

    Cash snapshot

    Pull today’s bank balance. Start the 13-week rolling forecast skeleton: opening cash, known payroll dates, rent, large AP. Don’t perfect it—frame it.

  3. 13:00

    Baseline O2C

    Walk one T&M and one milestone deal from work → invoice → cash. Timestamp the stages using the O2C map. Note where AI usage waits to be coded to a job.

  4. 15:00

    Measure DSO & overdue

    Ending AR, count-back DSO, AR aging by client, unbilled WIP list. Build tomorrow’s chase list from anything past terms. Template: DSO + aging.

  5. 16:30

    Close the day

    Write: what you know, what you don’t, who owes you a file by Friday. Tick what you can on the first-30 checklist. Schedule the AI/software spend inventory for this week—not today.
